How to Vote in Austin, Texas

New to voting? We will walk you through it, step by step.

You do not need to know anything yet. We will explain every word.

This guide was updated for the 2026 elections. If you are reading it in a later year, some dates and rules may have changed — always check the official links.

Your voting checklist

Voting takes a few steps. Follow them in order, or jump to the one you need.

  1. 1 Check that you can vote
  2. 2 Find out your county
  3. 3 Register (sign up) to vote in your county
  4. 4 Learn the Election Days
  5. 5 See who or what you can vote for
  6. 6 Decide who or what to vote for
  7. 7 Make your plan to vote
  8. 8 Vote!

Good to know

Getting ready is not the same as voting. Checking, registering, and looking at your ballot all come first. You have really voted only after you cast your ballot in Step 8.

Start with Step 1

This is not a government website. For official information, always check your county elections office or the Texas Secretary of State.
