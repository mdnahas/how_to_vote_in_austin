About This Site

We built this to help our neighbors vote. Voting can feel confusing the first time, so we made a simple guide. This site is paid for by the Michael Nahas campaign, but the goal here is just to help everyone vote, no matter who you support.

This is not a government website. For official information, always check your county elections office or the Texas Secretary of State.
