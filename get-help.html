Get More Help

These are trusted places to get help. Official sites have the final word. Some pages may be in English or Spanish only.

Official — the final word

  • VoteTexas.gov — the Texas Secretary of State’s voter site: register, check if you are registered, ID rules, and dates.
  • Texas election dates.

Your county elections office

Your county runs your elections. Find yours:

  • Travis County (most of Austin): votetravis.gov
  • Williamson County (some north areas): wilcotx.gov/elections
  • Hays County (some southwest areas): hayscountytx.gov/elections
  • Bastrop County (nearby areas): bastropvotes.org
  • Caldwell County (nearby areas): Caldwell County Elections

Not sure which county is yours? Go back to Step 2: Which county do you live in?

See what’s on your ballot

As an election gets close, your county posts a sample ballot on its elections website (linked above).

Neutral help to decide

  • Vote411 and the League of Women Voters Austin Area — voter guides that explain the choices without taking sides. Offered in English, Spanish, Chinese, and Vietnamese.

Language help

Voting materials in this area come in English and Spanish. Bilingual poll workers are in many precincts, but are not guaranteed at every location. You may bring someone to help you vote.
