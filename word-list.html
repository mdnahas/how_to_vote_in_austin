Word List

Simple meanings for the words on this website.

Register / Registration
Signing up to vote, like making an account. You do it once.
County
The local office that runs your elections. Your home address decides which one is yours.
Precinct
Your assigned voting area or spot.
Polling place
The building where you vote.
Vote center
A polling place where any voter in the county may vote (used in Travis County).
Ballot
Your list of choices.
Ballot measure
A question where voters decide something directly, instead of picking a person. A proposition and a bond are both ballot measures.
Proposition
A ballot measure that is a yes-or-no question about a rule.
Bond
A ballot measure that lets the government borrow money to build something, like a road, school, or park.
Primary
The “tryouts,” where each party picks its player.
General election
The main November election.
Runoff
A second, “overtime” vote when nobody wins clearly.
Early voting
Voting on days before Election Day.
Vote by mail
Voting with a paper ballot sent to you — only if you qualify.
Endorsement
When a person, group, or newspaper says publicly who they think you should vote for.
Voter ID
The ID you show to vote; your “ticket in.”
Provisional ballot
A backup ballot you can cast if there is a question about your registration. It counts once the question is cleared up.
Secretary of State
The person who runs elections in Texas.
